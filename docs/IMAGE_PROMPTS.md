# Image prompt pack: the 60 key-page photos

The first 60 photos for the site: the home banners, category tiles, edits, stories, New & Next brand launches and pop-ups. They are the pictures a visitor meets first. Every shot below matches a real slot on the site (the slot, the aspect ratio and what the picture has to show come from the site's own data), so the finished set drops straight in. The ~130 product photos come after these.

## How to use it

1. Open your image-generation site and pick its most realistic photo model. Set the **aspect ratio** shown for the shot.
2. Paste the shot's prompt (the look is already inside every prompt, so there is nothing to join). If the site has a *negative prompt* box, paste the one below it.
3. Make **3 or 4 versions** of each shot and keep the best one. Hands, text and cropped heads are the usual reasons to reject (checklist below).
4. Generate at the biggest size the site offers; upscale 2x if the long side is under the size in the table. Save as JPG or PNG, **named exactly like the shot** (for example `vrisa-wide.jpg`).
5. Send the folder or zip. I cut every slot from them, you check contact sheets, and it goes on staging first.

`image-prompts.csv` has the same 60 rows (file name, ratio, size, negative prompt, prompt) for a spreadsheet or a site with bulk generation.

## The look (already inside every prompt)

**Shots with people**

```
Premium editorial fashion photograph for an Indian online fashion store. Soft natural daylight, warm neutral palette (cream, sand, stone) with occasional accents of raspberry, marigold, emerald and indigo. True-to-life colour, fine natural skin texture, crisp realistic fabric detail, shallow depth of field, shot on a full-frame camera with an 85mm lens. Calm, uncluttered composition with generous clean negative space. South Asian people with natural, relaxed expressions. No text, letters, logos, signage or watermarks anywhere in the frame.
```

**Shots of objects and interiors**

```
Premium editorial still-life photograph for an Indian online lifestyle store. Soft natural window light, warm neutral palette (cream, sand, terracotta, oatmeal) with occasional accents of raspberry, marigold, emerald and indigo. True-to-life colour, crisp realistic material texture, shallow depth of field, shot on a full-frame camera with a 50mm lens. Calm, uncluttered composition with generous clean negative space. No people. No text, letters, logos, labels or watermarks anywhere in the frame.
```

**Negative prompt** (paste into the negative-prompt box if the site has one)

```
text, lettering, logo, brand name, watermark, signature, caption, border, frame, collage, split screen, cartoon, illustration, 3D render, plastic skin, airbrushed skin, deformed hands, extra fingers, extra limbs, distorted face, cropped head, celebrity likeness, harsh flash, HDR, oversaturated colours, lens flare, motion blur
```

Aspect ratios: 16:9 x 25, 4:5 x 20, 3:2 x 12, 4:3 x 3.  Sizes to aim for: 16:9 = 2560 x 1440 px, 4:5 = 2048 x 2560 px, 3:2 = 2400 x 1600 px, 4:3 = 2400 x 1800 px (at least; bigger is fine).

**Why most banners say "left 45% empty":** the site writes its headline over the left of every wide picture, and cuts a tall 4:5 version from the same frame, so the person has to stand right of centre with calm wall to their left, and fit inside the middle of the frame.

## If the site limits the length of a prompt

Prompts run from about 600 to 1,070 characters. If a site stops at 1,000, delete the sentence that starts "True-to-life colour" from the look in that prompt; the rest still works.

## Reject a picture if

- any readable text, logo or brand name appears (on clothes, bags, packaging, signs);
- hands have the wrong number of fingers, or a head, hand or foot is cut by the frame;
- the left 45% of a wide shot has something in it (a plant, a shadow pattern, a second person);
- the garment is not the one in the brief (a kurta set that came out as a dress, a saree with the wrong drape);
- the skin looks plastic, or the face looks like a recognisable celebrity;
- a child's face appears anywhere (the Kids tile is garments only).
